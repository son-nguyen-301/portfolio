## Name every item when asking Son to confirm something
class: correction

When asking Son to confirm or choose, list each item by name, say in one line what it is, and say exactly what decision is needed (for example "keep or drop from the public Lab page"). Never write a bare "confirm the list".

**Why:** Son corrected this on 2026-11-10: "Confirm what? Please be specific next time."
