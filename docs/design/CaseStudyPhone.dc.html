Multi-currency budgets and cards for a business spending app

As the only frontend developer on the team, I built budgets and cards that work in many currencies under one shared limit.

Role
Senior Frontend Developer, the only one on the team

The problem

A budget could hold only one currency. To spend in SGD and USD on the same thing, a company had to make two budgets with the same name.

Cards are attached to budgets, so the same problem spread to cards.
