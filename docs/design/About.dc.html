About

I have built web apps with Vue since 2019

I started as a frontend developer, then led a frontend team at XTEL, where I trained 10 engineers in Vue and Nuxt.

Since then I built the website for Terminal3, a game payments product at Paymentwall. I worked on cards, budgets and bills at Aspire, a Singapore fintech. I also set up a large online shop for a New Zealand company, and I lead the developers who build on it.

I care about code that the next person can pick up, about catching the edge cases before they reach users, and about saying early when something will be late. I work in English every day with teams in Singapore and New Zealand.
